## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Project name: HAREVEST HUB

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
HarvestHub is a digital marketplace designed to connect farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, supermarkets and other bulk buyers.

Through this platform, farmers can register and list their agricultural products along with their available quantities, prices and expected harvest dates. Buyers can browse available products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.

The platform will include the following features:

1.Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Connection: Connects farmers with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing their dependence on multiple intermediaries.

2.Smart Product Matching: Matches buyers' requirements with available products from nearby farmers.

3.Collective Bulk Ordering: Combines products from multiple small farmers to fulfil large orders, helping them reach more buyers.

4.AI-Based Demand Forecasting: Analyses previous orders and seasonal trends to estimate future demand for agricultural products.

5.Smart Logistics Support: Groups nearby orders and helps coordinate transportation to reduce delivery costs and delays.

6.Price Transparency: Allows farmers and buyers to view product prices and compare available offers.

The main feature that makes HarvestHub different is its collective bulk-ordering system. It allows small farmers to combine their produce and fulfil larger orders, even when they cannot supply the required quantity individually.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be Used:

1.Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript will be used to create a simple and user-friendly interface.

2.Backend: Python Flask will handle the application logic and user requests.

3.Database: MySQL will store farmer details, product information, buyer details and order records.

4.Data Analysis: Python and Pandas will be used to analyse product demand and order history.

5.AI Integration: Scikit-learn will be used to develop a basic demand prediction model using historical order data.

6.Mapping Support: OpenStreetMap will help display farmer, buyer and delivery locations.

Methodology and Process for Implementation:

1.Develop a registration and login system for farmers and buyers.

2.Create a farmer dashboard where farmers can add products, quantities, prices and expected harvest dates.

3.Develop a buyer dashboard where customers can browse products and place orders.

4.Implement a product-matching system to connect farmers with suitable buyers.

5.Develop a collective ordering feature that combines products from multiple farmers to fulfil bulk orders.

6.Introduce a basic demand prediction system using sample historical data.

7.Add logistics support to group nearby orders and suggest suitable delivery routes.

8.Test the application using sample farmer and buyer accounts to check its functionality.

## Feasibility and Viability

Analysis of Feasibility:

1.The application can be developed using Python Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL.

2.The initial prototype does not require expensive hardware.

3.Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones and computers.

4.Basic product matching and bulk-order management can be developed using simple programming logic.

5.The system can initially be tested with sample data before being introduced to real users.

Potential Challenges and Risks:

1.Some farmers may have limited experience using digital platforms.

2.Product availability and market prices may change frequently.

3.Coordinating transportation between different farmers and buyers may be challenging.

4.Internet connectivity may be limited in some rural areas.

5.Demand predictions may be less accurate when sufficient historical data is not available.

Strategies for Overcoming Challenges:

1.Provide a simple and easy-to-use interface.

2.Introduce local-language support in future versions.

3.Allow farmers to regularly update product availability and prices.

4.Group nearby orders to improve transportation coordination.

5.Start demand prediction with available sample data and improve the model as more order information becomes available.

6.Allow farmers and buyers to confirm orders and delivery arrangements manually.

The platform can initially be tested with a small group of farmers and buyers. Based on their feedback, improvements can be made before expanding to larger markets.
